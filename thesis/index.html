Open research thesis

[NEW] LLM-Powered Digital Brains for Smart Data Platforms
Master - Research thesis
Field: Business Intelligence and Data Mining
Status: Open
Abstract: The growing complexity of data-intensive applications and platforms calls for intelligent solutions that make data accessible to users without requiring advanced technical expertise. Our research explores the development of “digital brains”: smart data platforms in which Large Language Models (LLMs) and Knowledge Graphs work together to assist users throughout the data lifecycle - from discovering, exploring, and querying data to interpreting results and supporting decision-making. Particular attention is given to Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation (GraphRAG), which combines the natural-language capabilities of LLMs with the structured and contextual knowledge represented in graphs. Thesis projects may address different components of these platforms, including natural-language interfaces for data warehouses, KG construction, GraphRAG architectures, data-agent design, and the evaluation of system accuracy, usability, and reliability. Research can range from the design and optimization of a specific component to the development and assessment of complete solutions in real-world settings. Applications span question answering over enterprise data warehouses, research-oriented data and knowledge platforms, and practical projects developed with local companies, particularly in the fitness and agricultural sectors.
Contact: Prof. Matteo Golfarelli, Dr. Enrico Gallinucci, Dr. Matteo Francia

[NEW] Data modeling and cost modeling on Cloud-based NoSQL Databases
Master - Research thesis
Field: Big Data
Status: Open
Abstract: Data modeling in NoSQL databases follows an aggregate-oriented philosophy, which involves structuring data collections based on the workload—standing in clear contrast to relational modeling, which is domain-oriented and almost completely agnostic to the queries that will be implemented. The optimization of performances and costs of a NoSQL databases build on the design of data models that are consistent with the workload and that minimize the consumption of resources. In this context, it is crucial to understand the query mechanisms and cost models of the NoSQL databases made available by cloud providers and to effectively predict the cost of the adoption of different data models. Thesis oppurtunities in this field can cover several aspects of this broad topic, including (i) the development of algorithms for the automatic generation of data models for NoSQL databases, (ii) the design and implementation of cost models for cloud-based NoSQL databases, to evaluate costs in terms of performance, storage, monetary expenses, and carbon footprint.
Contact: Dr. Enrico Gallinucci

Open thesis within companies

[NEW] Applications and Artificial Intelligence for Industrial Data Collection, Analysis, and Reporting (@Fameccanica)
Master - Project thesis
Field: Big Data, Data Mining
Status: Open
Abstract: The increasing digitalization of manufacturing processes and the adoption of Industry 4.0 technologies are generating ever-growing volumes of data from industrial plants, automated production lines, and robotic cells. The ability to collect, analyze, and leverage this information has become a key factor in improving operational efficiency, supporting decision-making processes, and driving continuous improvement. The objective of this thesis is to analyze and identify innovative software solutions, with a particular focus on Artificial Intelligence, for the collection, processing, analysis, and reporting of data generated by industrial plants and robotic cells. The study will evaluate tools and methodologies capable of integrating data from both production systems and the verification, testing, and commissioning activities carried out by process engineers and specialized technicians. The ultimate goal is to transform the collected information into meaningful performance indicators and reports that support operational monitoring, performance management, and data-driven decision-making.
Contact: Davide Pugliese

Sviluppo di algoritmi di Machine Learning per il riconoscimento di fenomeni agricoli (@Winet srl).
Bachelor/Master - Research thesis
Field: Machine Learning and Data Mining
Status: Open
Abstract: L’agricoltura di precisione richiede strumenti avanzati per il monitoraggio continuo e automatico delle condizioni colturali e ambientali. L’utilizzo di dati multisorgente (es. immagini, sensori meteo e ambientali, trappole intelligenti) rappresenta una risorsa chiave per la comprensione della dinamica dei sistemi agricoli. La presente tesi si propone di sviluppare e valutare algoritmi di machine learning con particolare riferimento all’identificazione di insetti, alla stima della fase fenologica delle colture e al rilevamento di anomalie ambientali. L’obiettivo finale della tesi è fornire soluzioni algoritmiche scalabili e affidabili a supporto dei sistemi decisionali in ambito agricolo.
Contact: Prof. Matteo Golfarelli Dr. Matteo Francia

Business Intelligence presso l’Istituto Nazionale di Fisica Nucleare
Master - Project thesis
Field: Business Intelligence
Status: Open
Abstract: L’INFN cerca laureandi con possibilita’ di borsa di studio per lo sviluppo/evoluzione della propria infrastruttura di Business Intelligence. Le tecnologie coinvolte sono molteplici: Jasper, Tibco. Le tematiche vanno dallo sviluppo di reportistica, alla reportistica in tempo reale, all’utilizzo di sistemi NoSQL. 1) Progettazione e sviluppo di modelli dati multidimensionali orientati all’analisi visiva (dashboard e report dinamici) da produrre con strumenti di Business Analytics avanzati basati su un servizio cloud Microsoft Power BI riservato per INFN. 2) Progettazione e sviluppo su infrastruttura microservice-oriented (Spring based) di librerie e componenti java dedicati all’ampliamento dell’attuale architettura di ETL (Extract Transform and Load). L’obiettivo é intercettare, filtrare ed elaborare dati provenienti da sorgenti NoSQL( MongoDB) implementate secondo il modello microservizi. 3) Progettazione e realizzazione di modelli dati orientati all’analisi statistica con scopo predittivo, basati su dati di settore amministrativo dell’INFN (Gare e acquisti). Un possibile obiettivo é identificare le categorie di acquisto (prodotti e servizi) che nel tempo abbiano subito o meno maggiori fluttuazioni di mercato ed il loro impatto compleassivo sul budget dell’ente.
Contact: Prof. Matteo Golfarelli, Prof. Alessandra Lumini
